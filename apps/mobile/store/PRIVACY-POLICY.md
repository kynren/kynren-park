This policy explains how the **Kynren – The Storied Lands** mobile app ("the app", "we", "us")

We built the app to be **offline-first** and to collect as little as possible. You can plan a day,
view the map and browse show times **without an account** and **without sharing your location**.

## 1. What we collect, and why

| Data | When | Why (purpose) | Legal basis (UK GDPR) |
|------|------|---------------|-----------------------|
| **Email address, name** | If you create an account or sign in | Authenticate you; attach your tickets, orders and saved plans | Performance of a contract |
| **Precise location (GPS)** | Only while the map is open and only if you grant permission | Show where you are on the park map and calculate walking distances/times | Consent |
| **In-park presence (your coordinates sent to us)** | Only when signed in **and** location is granted | Let park staff see approximate live footfall for safety and crowd management | Consent (you can decline location) |
| **Push notification token** | If you allow notifications | Send show reminders and important park alerts | Consent |
| **Bookings & tickets** (ticket type, visit date, party size, QR token) | When you hold or add a ticket, or link a ticket order to your email address | Provide and validate your digital ticket | Performance of a contract |
| **Locale & accessibility preferences** | When you set them | Show the right language and accessibility information | Legitimate interests |
| **Aggregated usage** (e.g. which attractions are popular) | While using the app | Improve the app and plan operations — not used to identify you | Legitimate interests |

**Stored only on your device (never sent to us):** your favourites, your "seen" list, cached map,
schedule and ticket data for offline use. This lives in the app's local storage and is removed when
you delete the app.

## 2. What we do **not** do

- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, and we do **not** use third-party
  advertising or ad identifiers.
- We do **not** sell or rent your personal data.
- We do **not** collect payment card details in the app.
- We do **not** run your location in the background — it is read only while the map screen is open.

## 3. Who we share it with (processors)

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under contract:

- **Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)** — delivery of push notifications to your phone. It receives your
  device's notification token and the text of the notification.
- **Smeetz, our ticketing platform** — the source of your ticket orders. When you link tickets to your
  email address, we look the order up with them and keep a copy of the order so the app can show it offline.
- **Our own servers, in the United Kingdom** — the backend API and database that store your account,
  tickets and preferences.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — app distribution and, if you choose, crash diagnostics you can
  control in your device settings.

We disclose personal data only where necessary to run the service, or where required by law.

## 4. Where data is processed

Your account, tickets and preferences are stored on our own servers in the **United Kingdom**. Google
(push notifications) may process the notification token and message outside the UK, in which case it relies
on the safeguards it offers for UK personal data **[CONFIRM: UK-US Data Bridge certification or the
International Data Transfer Addendum, from Google's current terms]**.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account, tickets and orders:** for as long as your account is active, then for the period we are
  required to keep transaction records **[CONFIRM: period]**, after which it is deleted or anonymised.
  Deleting your account in the app removes your name, email, preferences, notification tokens and the
  links to your ticket orders straight away, and keeps only anonymous booking records.
- **Location/presence:** the last position the app sent us is cleared automatically, by a job that runs
  every 15 minutes, once it is more than 6 hours old, so it does not outlast your visit. It is only used to
  show staff roughly how many guests are in the park. Apart from that, your location stays on your phone.
- **Backups:** our database backups are kept for up to 14 days and are then deleted, so deleted data can
  remain in a backup for that long.
- **On-device data:** removed when you delete the app.

## 6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your
personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (for example by turning off
location or notification permissions in your device settings). To exercise any right, contact us at
**[privacy@kynren.com]**. You also have the right to complain to the UK **Information Commissioner's
Office** (ico.org.uk).

knowingly create accounts for children under 13 without appropriate parental involvement.
